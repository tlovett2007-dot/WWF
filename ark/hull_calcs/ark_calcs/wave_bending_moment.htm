How strong did the Ark have to be?

If the wooden hull of Noah's ark was in the open sea, it must be built to a certain minimum strength. Based on the worst seas of today, the first thing to check on any ship design is the bending strength along the hull. A ship riding over large waves experiences bending forces (moment or torque) that flex the hull up and down along its length (hogging and sagging). Without adequate strength and rigidity, the ark could leak or break when it meets high seas. For a starting point, we will investigate the required hull strength for an ark riding modern ocean waves.
