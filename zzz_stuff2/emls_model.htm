Here are the replies to Tim's questions:

Tim: 1. Assume water level rises, then peaks and falls. Emil: obviously. Yet this is less relevant for our model and therefore Should look as close as possible to any other similar animation Presenting The same stage ("inundatory" in tas walker's model) within the other Exhibits. What counts in this stage is the accumulation of soft Sediments That will subsequently become lithified (bound).

Tim: is mountain building separate from karst formation? Emil: it certainly is but it does not represent the initiation of Karsting Processes. What i believe happened is that once the sediments started Lithifying (diagenesis) they also started being dissolved - essentially By The same solutions that bound the grains together only that this time Their Ph changed. This could have already happened in the early stages of the Flood. It was nt the conduits that mattered at this point but rather Very Large voids which became filled with hydrothermal solutions. They will Supply more karsting solution later on, when tectonics started seriously Affecting the sediments. This would essentially happen during the uplift Of Continents (for the platform-type limestones) and the mountain chains (for The alpine karst). Folding and especially faulting will create further Connections between such big fluid reservoirs so that conduit-type karst Forms. As the uplift continues, some of the reservoirs will end up above The Base levels and their hyperactive solutions will try to drain towards The Base levels (rivers or even the ocean when close enough) creating the Multi-stage (level) cave systems.

Tim: how does your submerged karst correspond to baum's tectonic plate Buoyancy at the end of the flood? Emil: let it be said that i am not entirely following jb's model here. What Matters for my model is the deepening of the ocean basins (both by the Formation of the deep ocean trenches and by the shrinking of the Initially Hot new ocean bottom). If it was positive isostatic movements that Caused Water to run off the landmasses or the deepening of the ocean basins and Plate collision (and the resulting orogenies) is another issue. Again, i Don't mind making this animation match others that use jb's model. The Sheet Flow of the flood waters towards the oceans (in the "recessive" stage) Would Be the one that rapidly exposed the karst inside the emerging rocks.

Tim: what is water level doing at what parts of the karst formation? (i.e. Absolute water level - i need to correlate this frame to frame) esp - When Is the flood peak?

Emil: until stage 2 in my pp the water level has little relevance. In Stage 2 it is the massive erosion that exposes the fluid-filled reservoirs That is Important first, then the local/regional base levels i.e. the lowest River, The nearest sea or ocean which would force the downdrain of the Reservoirs And the formation of multi-stage cave passages. Keep in mind that this Is More or less a balance game between the retreating ocean waters and the Uprising continents/mountain chains. In fact the latter will cover most Of The cave passage formation whilst the former will have more to do with The Opening of the intraformational reservoirs and incipient conduits to the Surface. As for the peak of the flood, again, as far as i am concerned Anytime after the future cave-bearing sediments have been laid down is Ok; Just make it synch with other exhibits.
