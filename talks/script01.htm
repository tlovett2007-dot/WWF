ZOOMING IN ON NOAH'S ARK

If a bunch of engineering people come along, textbook in hand, wanting to tell you what Noah's Ark should look like, what would you think?

Oh yeah. God's ways are higher than our ways...(Isaiah 55:9.)

or Hasn't God made foolish the wisdom of this world? (1 Cor 1:20)

What value is there is attempting to reverse engineer Noah's Ark. I call it forensic guessing. It's a loose science - a bit like geology. 

I'll be the first to admit we are guessing in places, but let's say we are attempting an educated guess. 

The ground rules are as usual - we start with what the Bible tells us and then propose a model, then test the model however we can- predictions, empirical tests etc

So here we are with Noah's Ark. We've got dimensions - Length 300 cubits, width 50, height 30

We've got a door in the side that God shut, we have a light near the top, there's 3 levels...it's made of gopher wood, covered inside and out with pitch...there's many animal nests inside...

And it carried the ancestors of all the world's mammals, birds and reptiles, with all sorts of food for them.

There's a crew of 8... 4 men and 4 women.

We also know that Noah had a window he could open and he was able to remove the covering of the ark, and they got out somehow...

..and that's it!

 

So...

What did it look like? or more correctly...What is our best guess?

 

At the basic level, we could ask...

  • How long is a cubit? 
  • What shape was the Ark? 
  • What is gopher wood?
  • How many animals?
  • What was the window like?
  • How was the door made?
  • What tools did they have?

 

Digging deeper, how do we answer questions like...

  • Can it have the necessary stability and strength?
  • Would lighting and ventilation work?
  • How could the crew care for the animals?
  • What about structural details like joining of wood?
  • How bad was the weather?

Let's get into it a bit more...

  • Have we accounted for animals in storm conditions?
  • How do the animals drink water?
  • Is there enough light on the lowest level?
  • What is the design intent of a long hull?
  • Can Woodmorape's 15000 animals really fit?
